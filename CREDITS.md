## Textures

Modified maps from [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com), published under [CC0](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/):

- [Foliage001](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Foliage001)
- [Ground037](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Ground037)
- [Bark014](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Bark014)
- [LeafSet019](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=LeafSet019)
- [LeafSet024](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=LeafSet024)

Maps are resized, composited, and re-encoded for the game.
Other surface textures are procedural.

## Announcer

Speech was rendered with [Kokoro-82M v1.0](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M), voice af_heart, using [kokoro-onnx](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx).
Recordings are trimmed, normalized, and encoded as Ogg and MP3 audio.
The model weights use Apache-2.0 and the synthesis runtime uses MIT.
Only finished recordings are bundled; those tool licenses describe production dependencies, not a separate license grant for the game.
